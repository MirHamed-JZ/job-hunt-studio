# Application tracker

One row per posting. Statuses: `Scored`, `Do Not Apply`, `On hold`, `Ready to submit`, `Applied`, `Followed up`, `Screen`, `Interview`, `Offer`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`, `Closed (no response)`. Follow-ups: {{applications.followup_days}} days after applying, after a follow-up and after a screen, unless another date is given; none once a row is closed.
