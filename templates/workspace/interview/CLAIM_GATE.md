# Claim gate

A skill from Ledger `[skills-gaps]` goes on your resume or LinkedIn only after you have built something with it and passed `/job-hunt-studio:interview-drill <skill>`: a specific example from your own project and sound reasoning on at least 4 of 5 questions. Then `/job-hunt-studio:update-ledger` moves it to `[skills-claimed]`, and any resume change becomes a new version through review and approval.

Written by interview-drill, one row per drill, newest last.
