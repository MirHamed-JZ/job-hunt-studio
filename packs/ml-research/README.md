# ml-research pack

For machine learning engineers, applied scientists and research engineers.

- [`keywords.yaml`](keywords.yaml): job titles, the terms employers ask for (used for scoring and the keyword report, never added to a resume unless the Ledger supports them), buzzwords to avoid, and what the resume reviewer weighs for role fit.
- [`TOPICS.md`](TOPICS.md): 16 interview areas for `mock-interview`, with probe styles and readings: 7 core areas (fundamentals, statistics, deep learning, evaluation, system design, MLOps, coding) and 9 field areas (LLMs, post-training, inference, retrieval, agents, ranking and recommendation, vision, speech, data and SQL).
